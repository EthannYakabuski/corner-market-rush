## Title

Corner Market Rush

## Short description

Stock the shelves, serve the baskets, and make your little corner shine.

## Long description

A little shop. A lively rush.

The awning is up, the first basket is through the door, and someone needs the last apple on the shelf. Welcome to Corner Market Rush, a 3D neighborhood shop game about the small decisions that keep a busy corner running.

STOCK SMART, SERVE WITH CARE
Customers gather the products on their shopping lists. You keep the shelves stocked and send the clerk to check out completed baskets. A nearly empty shelf or a waiting regular: which job comes next? Tap a shelf or customer, queue one more task and keep a clear view of stock, reserves and patience.

36 SHIFTS WITH NEIGHBORHOOD CHARACTER
Learn the rhythm at Maple Corner, catch the Morning Bakery crowd and keep a welcoming light in the Rainy Window. Market Square, Tram Stop Express and Lantern Night add fuller baskets and new service pressures. Six chapters contain six authored shifts each, introducing mixed orders, stock deliveries and optional mastery goals.

GET TO KNOW YOUR REGULARS
Mina likes a bright apple. Theo is taking bread home. Ivy is bringing something green to the table. Recognizable customers give every basket a little personality while you find a steady service rhythm.

GROW YOUR CORNER
Earn coins and manager experience through completed service. Improve shelf capacity, reserves and shop decor, then return for mastery stars and better unassisted combos. Try untimed practice when you want room to learn. Ordinary retries are free.

A FRESH LITTLE ROUTINE
Daily baskets, three daily goals and a seven-day welcome calendar offer more reasons to open the awning. Work toward 20 achievements and follow your served-customer, unassisted-combo and manager-experience records. Optional earned assistance can bring a reserve crate when it is available.

A WARM PLACE TO SPEND A SHIFT
Watch the clerk walk, pick up stock and serve among colorful shelves and a lively street backdrop. Original daytime, rush and evening music accompanies the changing mood. Portrait tap controls keep the choices close at hand, and local progress keeps your shop on this device.

Every basket tells a little story. Make yours a welcoming one.
