# Corner Market Rush — website copy

Candidate copy for the full first-pass game; verify the final build and HANDOFF before presenting these features as available. The planned Sunday Flower Market expansion is not part of this release.

## Hero

**A little shop. A lively rush.**

Open the awning on a welcoming 3D corner market. Keep the shelves stocked, check out complete baskets and find the rhythm of a neighborhood that knows your name.

**Primary call to action:** Play Corner Market Rush

Use this label only when the reviewed Android download or playable Web build is actually available. No live download or embed URL is supplied here.

## Three selling cards

### Every basket tells a little story

Six recognizable regulars bring their favorite groceries and a little personality through the door. Follow their baskets from the shelf to the checkout across 36 authored shifts.

### Stock smart, serve with care

A waiting basket, a nearly empty shelf and one clerk: choose the next job that matters. Clear tap targets, visible reserves and a cancellable queued task keep each decision understandable.

### Grow the corner everyone knows

Earn coins and manager experience, improve your shelves and reserves, and make the shop your own with decor growth. Daily baskets, daily goals and mastery stars offer a reason to open the awning again.

## A small piece of neighborhood lore

Theo stops in before the ride home. Mina picks out a bright apple. When rain dots the window or the evening lamps come on, the corner is still a familiar place to find what is missing from the table. Your job is to keep those small promises moving, one basket at a time.

## Inspiration

The welcoming character of a neighborhood grocery meets a focused service puzzle. Customers gather their lists while you decide how the clerk divides attention between shelves and checkout. The shop grows through attentive service, not unattended income.

## Technical and service copy

Made in Godot 4.5.1 with the Compatibility renderer. Portrait Android is the primary platform, with a Web export prepared as an additional way to play. Progress is saved locally. The Android integration includes Google Play Games and AdMob, configured with test advertising IDs and disabled production Play Games mappings in this first pass. Online records, production ads and final backend/signing setup require the owner's configuration and testing. Native-only services and earned assistance remain unavailable gracefully in Web; the game does not award a pretend ad reward.

Do not turn source/export support into a claim of physical-device, browser-interaction or audible-output validation; see the current handoff for actual evidence and limits. No Windows distribution is planned.
