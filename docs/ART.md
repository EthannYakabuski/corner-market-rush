# Illustrated artwork

The app icon and feature graphic are original promotional raster illustrations generated with the built-in image-generation tool on 2026-10-22. They are marketing art, not screenshots of gameplay. The game's actual scenery uses the separately credited Kenney models; illustration detail and lighting exceed the low-poly gameplay rendering.

The selected originals were technically resampled full-frame to the store sizes without cropping, compositing or semantic retouching. Original icon: 1254x1254; original feature: 1795x876. Final PNGs are RGB 512x512 and 1024x500. Both were visually inspected at full export size, the icon at 64x64 and the feature at 512x250. Title spelling, focal readability and distinct game mood were checked. Device/audio/gameplay QA is separate.

## App icon prompt

Use case: ads-marketing / illustrated mobile game app icon.
Asset: one finished raster app icon for "Corner Market Rush", 512 by 512 pixels, square opaque full bleed.
Create premium cheerful stylized 3D illustrated game key art. A friendly dark-skinned neighborhood shopkeeper in a teal-green apron holds one overflowing wicker grocery basket toward the viewer: a glossy red apple, two oranges, a golden loaf and leafy greens make a bold colorful foreground shape. The shopkeeper's expressive welcoming face and basket form one unified central silhouette occupying about 75 percent of the frame, with rounded toy-like low-poly proportions, beautifully shaded surfaces and lively human warmth. Behind are the curved corner of a cream shopfront, a coral striped awning, one warmly lit window and lush green potted leaves. Rich painterly lighting, gentle sunny rim light, warm wood/cream textures, lively coral and emerald palette, hand-crafted premium mobile-game illustration. Background is detailed enough to feel like a real inviting place but softened to preserve face and basket readability at 48 pixels. Composition uses a close three-quarter view with clear large shapes and all essential subject parts inside a 10 percent safe edge for rounded Android masks. No text, letters, numbers, logos, shopping-cart symbol, badge frame, watermarks, realistic photography, pixel art, flat vector geometry, screenshot or UI.

## Feature graphic prompt

Use case: ads-marketing / illustrated store feature graphic.
Asset: one finished wide raster feature image for the mobile game "Corner Market Rush", 1024 by 500 pixels, landscape, opaque full bleed.
Premium stylized 3D illustrated mobile-game key art, lively and richly detailed with rounded toy-like low-poly proportions and painterly materials/light. A beautiful neighborhood corner grocery occupies the right two-thirds of the composition in a playful fixed oblique cutaway view: cream storefront and coral striped awning, warmly lit wood-and-tile interior, shelves of colorful groceries, glossy fruit crates, bread display, glass freezer and a real checkout register. A smiling dark-skinned shopkeeper in a teal-green apron is handing a full grocery basket to a happy older customer at the foreground checkout; a second customer with a short shopping list waits by the produce and one small restock crate sits near the shelves. These are a few clear readable characters, not a crowded mob. Planters, leafy street trees, cobblestone edge, string lights and a friendly neighboring facade give the location personality. Storytelling details support gathering baskets, keeping shelves full and serving a neighborhood, with warm approachable energy rather than frantic chaos. Morning sunlight, coral/cream/emerald palette with appetizing fruit accents, rich material shading and soft depth. Leave the left third as a calm cream-and-mint shop-sign area framed by a few leaves. Integrate only the exact title "CORNER MARKET RUSH" in three short expertly lettered lines there, bold dark teal with restrained coral accent and warm dimensional shading, readable at reduced size. No additional text. Compose natively at 1024:500; keep all important title and characters within a 6 percent safe edge. It must look like polished bespoke illustrative promotional art, not a flat logo, generic supermarket photo, pixel art or gameplay screenshot. No UI, phone frames, ratings, laurels, badges, watermarks or borrowed logos.
