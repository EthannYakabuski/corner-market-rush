# Illustrated artwork

The app icon and feature graphic are original promotional raster illustrations generated with the built-in image-generation tool on 2026-10-22. They are marketing art, not screenshots of gameplay. The game's actual scenery uses the separately credited Kenney models; illustration detail and lighting exceed the low-poly gameplay rendering.

The selected originals were technically resampled full-frame to the store sizes without cropping, compositing or semantic retouching. Original icon: 1254x1254; original feature: 1795x876. Final PNGs are RGB 512x512 and 1024x500. Both were visually inspected at full export size, the icon at 64x64 and the feature at 512x250. Title spelling, focal readability and distinct game mood were checked. Device/audio/gameplay QA is separate.

The shopkeeper's expressive welcoming face and basket form one unified central silhouette occupying about 75 percent of the frame, with rounded toy-like low-poly proportions, beautifully shaded surfaces and lively human warmth. Behind are the curved corner of a cream shopfront, a coral striped awning, one warmly lit window and lush green potted leaves. Rich painterly lighting, gentle sunny rim light, warm wood/cream textures, lively coral and emerald palette, hand-crafted premium mobile-game illustration. Background is detailed enough to feel like a real inviting place but softened to preserve face and basket readability at 48 pixels. Composition uses a close three-quarter view with clear large shapes and all essential subject parts inside a 10 percent safe edge for rounded Android masks. No text, letters, numbers, logos, shopping-cart symbol, badge frame, watermarks, realistic photography, pixel art, flat vector geometry, screenshot or UI.
